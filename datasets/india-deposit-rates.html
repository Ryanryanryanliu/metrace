India bank deposit rates

Posted fixed deposit rates for 22 Indian banks, rebuilt month by month from archived bank websites and rate cards. The Reserve Bank of India publishes bank-group averages. We found no vendor with this history bank by bank.

Banks
22: 8 public sector, 12 private, 2 small finance
Period
2021 to 2026; some banks from earlier
Rates
Every tenor on the bank's card; the one-year rate is the bucket that contains 365 days
Dates
The effective date the bank stated, and the capture date
Sources
Bank rate pages, rate-card PDFs and the data files behind them, as archived

One-year deposit rates and the repo rate

General public, retail deposits. Each step is a change seen on the bank's own page or rate card.

Banks passed on less of the cuts

In the 2022 hikes the median bank raised its one-year rate by 1.70 points against a 2.50 point rise in the repo rate (16 banks). By February 2026 the median bank had cut it by 0.55 points against a 1.25 point cut (16 banks). 11 of the 12 banks seen in both cycles passed on a smaller share of the cuts.

Change in the one-year rate from the last capture before each cycle to March 2023 (hikes) and to February 2026 (cuts), divided by the change in the repo rate. Banks with a capture from June 2024 on before the cuts.

Checks

  • First 2022 rate rises dated as in news reports for all 7 banks checked
  • 13 of 16 State Bank of India rate changes recovered against the bank's own history file, with no false dates
  • Old and new rates read from the bank's own "existing rate" columns where the page shows them

Used for

  • Bank equity research: deposit costs and margins by bank
  • Rate pass-through studies with exact dates
  • Competitive pricing for banks and fintech lenders
