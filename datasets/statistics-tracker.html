Official statistics tracker

A dated record of official statistics that were suspended, delayed, redefined, removed or resumed, rebuilt from archived versions of statistics office websites.

Coverage
40 official sources in 30 countries
Period
2015 to 2026, rebuilt from web archives
Event types
Suspension, delay, methodology change, removal, resumption
Evidence
An archived office page and an independent source for every event
Delivery
CSV and Parquet files

First release: 68 events in 17 countries. Suspensions 17, delays 22, methodology changes 21, resumptions 7, removals 1.
