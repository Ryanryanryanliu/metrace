China government arrears

Since 2021, Chinese government agencies must publish each March how many contracts with small firms they left unpaid past the deadline, and the amount. We collect these reports, merge copies posted on more than one site, and record who reports, what they report and who stays silent.

Coverage
Guangdong province, every government site in the provincial search; other provinces next
Period
Data years 2021 to 2025
Reports
486 distinct reports from at least 111 agencies
Legal basis
Regulations on Ensuring Payments to Small and Medium Enterprises, State Council Orders 728 (2020) and 802 (2025)
Delivery
CSV files, refreshed after each 31 March deadline
