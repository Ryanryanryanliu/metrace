Synopsis Notes

The same film, two languages: which details do you encounter first? A close reading of official bilingual descriptions.

Read the five-page issue

Five readings, including one alignment

The Shameless, Plan 75, War Pony, The Village Next to Paradise and Tirailleurs. Five editorial selections from 29 complete English–French pairs in a fixed 38-film frame.

The Shameless

A relationship detail

The French synopsis explicitly describes a mother–daughter confrontation. The English account describes romance and escape without naming that confrontation.

War Pony

Different emphasis on livelihood

Both versions include economic activity. The French wording makes meeting basic expenses more explicit, while the English wording foregrounds personal advancement.

Tirailleurs

A comparison that aligns

Both versions describe a father joining his forcibly recruited son, facing war together and seeking to bring him home. The comparison also records shared information.

A tool for reading and editing

These cards make specific details visible: relationships, livelihood, policy assistance and protective intentions. They can help editors and researchers check how a short description directs attention.

The films come from the 2022 and 2024 Cannes Un Certain Regard editions. Those are festival years; the retrospective pages were retrieved in 2026. Original publication dates, translation direction and revision history are unknown.

The cases compare the synopses, not complete films or national cultures. They do not estimate the prevalence of differences, grade translation quality or establish a trend over time. Reviews were performed by AI agents against saved original passages.
