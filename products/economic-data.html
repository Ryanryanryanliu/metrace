Brazil · Publication plan change

Brazil: a census postponement is a plan change

An archived IBGE English homepage records an announcement dated 17 March 2020 that moves the planned 2020 census to 2021. This case records that contemporary plan change, not the eventual completion year.

Japan · Methodology and comparability

Japan: survey forms can affect year-on-year comparability

Japan’s Statistics Bureau explains on 9 March 2018 that half the household sample began using new diary forms in January. It warns of possible discontinuity in year-on-year changes and contributions, supplies a propensity-score/inverse-probability adjustment, and records a retrospective recalculation on 8 February 2019.

Greece · Specific release suspension

Greece: a flash estimate is suspended while a later release stays scheduled

On 4 August 2017, ELSTAT suspends the GDP flash estimate normally released 45 days after a quarter. The planned 14 August Q2 flash estimate is cancelled, while the same notice keeps provisional Q2 data scheduled for 1 September.

South Africa · Resumption with series gap

South Africa: resumed publication retains a documented gap

Stats SA states that monthly insolvency statistics resumed on 21 July 2026 with June data and a series starting in January 2023. The article identifies a September 2021–December 2022 gap and says the new release is not a continuous extension of the old series. Company liquidation statistics had continued.

China · Catch-up publication

China: six reports keep reference quarters and page dates separate

Six People’s Bank of China pages for the urban depositor, entrepreneur and banker surveys cover Q3 and Q4 2023 and display 22 March 2024 as their publication date. An official-list snapshot from 9 July 2024 contains all six links. The inherited ledger groups the three series under one delayed-publication context.

China · Definition change

China: receivables labels mark two definition changes

The NBS industrial-profit release of 27 March 2019 replaces monthly accounts receivable with notes and accounts receivable. The 27 March 2020 release changes back to accounts receivable. Both explain the adjustments in relation to corporate financial-statement formats.

China · Breakdown discontinuation

China: a breakdown is removed while aggregate industrial statistics continue

NBS releases of 14 and 27 March 2019 explicitly remove the collective-enterprise breakdown from 2019 onward. Those releases still publish their respective aggregate industrial output and profit results.

Vietnam · New release verified

Vietnam: October CPI is a new reference month

Two official releases report national October 2026 CPI inflation of 5.09% year on year. The same publications separately report 0.20% month on month and a 4.57% ten-month average. September’s 5.08% belongs to another reference month; the October value is not a revision to it.

What this evidence can establish

  • The current panel is selected coverage, not exhaustive global monitoring.
  • The follow-up establishes one new release and no verified same-period numeric revisions; this is not a lifetime count.
  • Capture time establishes observation, not first public availability; date-only release plans do not establish intraday delays.
  • Historical examples preserve limits such as archived homepage text, inaccessible full releases and unresolved first-access times.
