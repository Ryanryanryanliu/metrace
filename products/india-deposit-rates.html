Axis Bank · Table-specific date interpretation

Axis: three dates in one archived rate card

An Axis PDF named for 8 June 2023 was archived on 10 June. Its first-page retail table states applicability from 18 May and quotes 6.75% for general deposits below INR 2 crore, one year to one year and four days. The 8 June date on page 3 belongs to deposits of INR 5 crore and above.

What this evidence can establish

  • The 188 accepted observations include repeat captures; they are not 188 distinct rate changes.
  • The scope is general-public domestic retail INR deposits; original tenor labels and amount thresholds remain authoritative.
  • Sparse archives do not identify the first adjustment or prove that a dated PDF quote was active on its capture date.
  • Comparisons are descriptive; causal monetary-policy pass-through, personalized returns and investment recommendations are outside scope.
