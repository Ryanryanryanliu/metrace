What this evidence can establish

  • This is an exploratory text comparison, not a cultural trend index or a translation-quality score.
  • Nine of the 38 frame entries lack a complete bounded English/French pair and remain unclassified.
  • The review reads all 29 preserved text pairs. Of their 58 full-page snapshot files, 42 were locally readable and reverified; 16 were not resident and were skipped. Every public case has both full snapshots verified.
  • The snapshots are saved web-tool text extractions, not original HTML. They were retrieved in November 2026; first publication dates, translation direction and past wording are unknown.
  • A detail not stated in an introduction may still appear in the film. No film-level fact checking or inference about editorial motives is made.
  • The case review was performed by AI agents, not certified by a human bilingual editor.
