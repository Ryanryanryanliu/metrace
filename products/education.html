Edinburgh · Documented continuity

Machine learning inside a required data science course

Across five observed editions, Edinburgh places INFR08030 in the second-year compulsory group. Its description includes supervised machine learning, inference and algorithmic bias. The separate INFR10086 Machine Learning course remains an option.

Edinburgh · Requirements and delivery status

A listed project option can have a non-delivery notice

Edinburgh lists SDP and EIP in a choose-20-credit project group through 2025/26, while the 2025/26 EIP page says it is not being delivered. In 2026/27, SDP is separately compulsory, with an approved Formula Student substitution route; EIP retains its non-delivery notice.

MIT · Description change

MIT teaching plans mention LLMs before the latest catalogue sentence

The official-linked Fall 2025 6.1040 course site already plans LLM-assisted design, coding and application agents, while the Fall 2025 Registrar description lacks that sentence. The verified 2026/27 central catalogue explicitly mentions LLM design and coding.

MIT · Description change

A machine learning description changes its organizing emphasis

MIT 6.3900 changes from prediction-oriented wording in 2023/24 to an optimization-oriented description in 2024/25, explicitly listing supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning. The core wording continues in the Fall 2025 Registrar archive and 2026/27 catalogue.

MIT · Applicability unresolved

An AI-list condition needs an applicability check

The verified 2026/27 central MIT 6-3 catalogue requires at least two AUS subjects and at least 12 units from its AI Subjects list. The departmental 6-3_2025 rules page, labelled for entrants from Fall 2025, still specifies Independent Inquiry constraints. These official source layers do not yet establish one rule for an individual student.

Carnegie Mellon · Stable category; changing options

CMU keeps the AI category while changing its options

Five observed CMU editions keep a requirement to choose one course from the AI category, with a minimum of 9 CMU units. The 2025/26 named list includes 07-280 and 10-301 and no longer lists 10-315; 15-281 is still listed then but absent in 2026/27, which includes 16-280.

Carnegie Mellon · Credit change

More units do not by themselves establish new content

CMU 15-122 and 15-150 each move from 10 units in 2022/23 to 12 units in 2023/24, remaining in the all-required CS core. Their main catalogue descriptions are unchanged after whitespace normalization and removal of separate metadata fields. Both retain 12 units through 2026/27.

UW–Madison · Requirement structure change

A broad mathematics list becomes two separate groups

UW–Madison CS BS changes its additional mathematics requirement from two courses in a broad list to one Linear Algebra choice and one Probability or Statistics choice in 2024/25. Both groups remain in 2025/26 and 2026/27.

UW–Madison · Description change

R becomes explicit in a statistics course description

UW–Madison STAT 324 adds an explicit R-programming statement in the 2024/25 description while retaining its earlier statistics topics. That statement remains in 2025/26 and 2026/27; the course stays an option in the CS mathematics requirement.

Waterloo · Rule clarification

An explicit alternative can document an existing permission

Waterloo BCS names CS 240 or CS 240E and CS 241 or CS 241E explicitly in its 2023/24 programme table. The 2022/23 course catalogue already allowed the enriched courses to replace the standard courses wherever required. The explicit alternatives persist through 2026/27.

Waterloo · Requirement structure change

Non-mathematics electives are reorganized in Waterloo BCS

The 2025/26 BCS still uses the earlier breadth-and-depth structure. The 2026/27 version organizes 4.0 elective units into a 1.0 + 1.0 + 2.0 distribution, including at least 1.0 unit at the 200 level or above and no double counting with the degree communication requirement. Both retain at least 5.0 non-mathematics units and two communication courses.

Toronto · Eligibility and credit constraints

Toronto changes which courses count within an unchanged total

The St George CS Major keeps its 3.0-credit Later years module while narrowing its 200-level CSC list in 2024/25 to CSC209H1, CSC299H1 and CSC299Y1. It also explicitly states two separate 0.5-credit caps, for CSC412H1/STA414H1 and CSC438H1/MAT309H1. These features remain after a later regrouping.

Toronto · Approved option change

An existing deep learning course gains a new completion role

Toronto adds CSC302H1 and CSC413H1 to the CS Major integrative inquiry-based activity list in 2023/24. Both courses already existed in 2022/23, when CSC413H1 already described deep learning. Their main descriptions are unchanged across that interval, and both remain listed as options through 2026/27.

What this evidence can establish

  • Selected official programme and course texts, not a census of all courses at these institutions.
  • Five institutions have five observed annual editions; MIT’s 2025/26 central annual programme rules remain a documented gap.
  • Catalogue wording and eligibility do not measure teaching, student uptake or learning outcomes.
  • Academic-year versions, entry cohorts and retrieval dates are distinct; credits stay in each institution’s own units.
