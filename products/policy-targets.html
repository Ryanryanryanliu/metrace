Inside the public preview

Austria · Target lowered

Austria lowers the national basic digital skills target

Austria’s original roadmap uses a maximum-100% formulation and also contains a generic 80% heading. The Commission’s 2024 assessment identifies the national target as 100%. The revised roadmap sets 80% for 2030, supporting a 20-percentage-point reduction with the original qualifier retained.

Finland · Target raised

Finland raises its SME digital intensity target

Finland’s revised roadmap explicitly raises the 2030 target for SMEs with at least basic digital intensity from 90% to 95%. The 2025 DIIv3 result is 94.0%, one percentage point below the revised target.

Estonia · New national targets

Estonia adds national KPI targets; an old percentage is not a baseline

The 2026 Commission report identifies 13 new national targets in Estonia’s 2025 roadmap. The older skills objective of 60% concerns above-basic skills by 2035; the new 80% concerns at least basic skills by 2030. They cannot be treated as a 20-point increase or a five-year acceleration. The revised roadmap also supplies a national cloud-adoption target.

Finland · New target; distinct indicators

Finland adds an FTTP target beside its VHCN target

Finland explicitly adds a 100% FTTP coverage target for 2030 in the revised roadmap. Its 2025 FTTP coverage is 80.0%, while VHCN coverage is 84.6%. The two indicators describe different network coverage measures and remain separate.

Finland · Definition comparison held

The same 75% can refer to a different indicator

The original target table specifies 75% for big-data adoption; the revised table specifies 75% for data analytics. The indicator and measurement scope changed, so equal numbers cannot establish an unchanged target on the same definition.

Estonia · Source conflict held

Estonia’s edge-node target remains unresolved

The 2026 summary table lists an Estonian edge-node target of 5, but the report narrative says no target was set and the 2025 roadmap’s national-target cell is a dash. The 2025 estimate of 28 nodes uses a new method. Both the target and cross-version outcome comparison remain held.

What this evidence can establish

  • Selected national roadmaps and progress reports; no comprehensive EU-country ranking.
  • National targets, EU collective targets, planned annual trajectories and observed results remain separate.
  • Numeric comparison depends on aligned scope and definition. An added target is not a zero-to-target increase.
  • All selected 2030 deadlines are still ahead. Current gaps do not establish final delivery, causes or political motives.
