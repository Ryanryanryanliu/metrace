Another saved record with the same company, generic name and package NDC is To Be Discontinued, with a June 1, 2026 discontinuation date. Presentation wording differs. What this evidence can establish

  • The 107 accepted records form a frozen cohort; they do not represent all US shortages or individual medicines.
  • Across this one observation pair, 94 raw records were identical and 13 differed only in annotation-array order; no substantive forecast or supply-field changes were found.
  • Three identity conflicts remain outside the accepted cohort; one accepted record retains a supply conflict that blocks recovery-outcome interpretation.
  • No real-world inventory validation, recovery rate, forecast accuracy or final recovery outcome is reported. Current API links may differ from saved historical evidence.
