About

Metrace measures what no one else can: numbers that are doubted, rare, hidden, not yet priced, or impossible to count before language models. We start with the economy and build toward culture, education and technology on one engine.

Founder

Xiaoran Liu is an economist who measures things with language models and checks the results against ground truth. PhD in Economics, Nanyang Technological University (December 2026). B.A. in Economics and Applied Mathematics, UC Berkeley. Earlier measurement work covers culture from song lyrics and ethnographies, the effect of ChatGPT on public knowledge across 46 platforms, and AI policy read from 11,643 government gazette documents.

Top 100 of more than 1,000 entries in Bridgewater's Forecasting the Future challenge. First place nationally in the Temasek and Financial Times Financial Challenge.
