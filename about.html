About

Metrace builds datasets that markets need and no one kept: what governments, banks and agencies published, changed or removed, with the date and the source.

Founder

Xiaoran Liu is an economist who measures things with language models and checks the results against ground truth. PhD in Economics, Nanyang Technological University (December 2026). B.A. in Economics and Applied Mathematics, UC Berkeley. Research on where generative AI is used and what it replaces, and on measuring culture and institutions from text.

Top 100 of more than 1,000 entries in Bridgewater's Forecasting the Future challenge. First place nationally in the Temasek and Financial Times Financial Challenge.
