How we measure

  1. Collect

    Preserve relevant official pages, archives and data files, including gaps in what can be retrieved.

  2. Compare

    Keep identity, definitions and document versions separate while looking for changes.

  3. Review

    Check selected claims against source passages and record what the evidence cannot establish.

  4. Deliver

    Publish research issues, evidence cards and documented samples with product-specific scope.
