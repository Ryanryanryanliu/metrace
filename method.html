Evidence and interpretation

Method

A useful data point needs an identity, a source and a defensible date. Every product states the scope of its evidence and review.

From source to finding

Start from a specific source and unit

Official releases, university catalogues, bank rate cards and shortage notices require different definitions. We record the institution, series or product identity before comparing versions. A page, a row and an underlying event are not interchangeable units.

Preserve evidence and timing

Where available, we retain source URLs, local snapshots and file hashes. A publisher’s displayed date, a statistical reference period, an archive capture and our retrieval time are separate fields. A later capture proves presence by that date; it does not prove first availability.

Read, challenge and narrow the claim

AI tools extract and compare source material. Selected findings receive separate source-based review; individual releases describe whether that review was independent coding, an informed challenge or a spot check. Review by AI agents is not human certification. Outside corroboration is stated where obtained, rather than assumed for every row.

Keep missing evidence visible

A failed request is an access gap, not proof of a discontinued statistic. A changed link is a candidate for investigation, not automatically a new event. An archived edition may omit intermediate revisions. We do not claim an exact historical state beyond the versions actually preserved.

Distinguish corrections from source changes

When our interpretation changes, the release records a correction. When new source evidence is recovered, its acquisition and historical dates remain distinct. Product releases state their current scope and unresolved gaps.

What the current checks establish

The inherited statistics-tracker retrospective test covered 22 in-scope known events: 17 had a same-series match and 14 had strict evidence showing the event itself. Detection rules and review scope were adjusted during development, so these are retrospective results, not performance on an untouched prospective test set.

The 47-row global discovery catalogue retains its earlier judgments. Economic Data Watch Issue 01 rechecks only seven selected cards. Its China module uses different review grades, and overlapping records are kept linked rather than added into a global total.

Education Issue 01 reviews all 13 evidence cards and spot-checks eight course records. The drug pilot has one prospective API baseline. Synopsis Notes has five editorial cases from a larger pairing exercise. None of these denominators is a universal accuracy measure.
